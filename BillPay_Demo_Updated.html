Frequently asked questions

What if a payment is pending?

The system should query the provider until a final result is returned. Never retry a purchase blindly.

When will a failed payment be refunded?

Once the provider confirms final failure, the system should post an idempotent refund entry to the wallet ledger.

Is this demo connected to VTpass?

No. All purchases and wallet funding are simulated locally.
